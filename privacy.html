Privacy policy

This policy describes the personal integrations operated by Eduardo Collazos under the Google OAuth application name n8n personal.

Data accessed and purpose

The integrations use only the Google permissions authorized by the account owner. Nest access can include home and room information, thermostat settings and sensor readings, camera streams, and motion or doorbell events. YouTube Music access supports the owner’s personal music functionality. OAuth credentials and tokens are used to maintain these authorized connections.

Storage and sharing

Integration credentials and operational data are processed by the owner’s personally managed applications and systems. Requests are exchanged with Google and the relevant service providers as needed to operate the integrations. This website does not display account data, credentials, device locations, camera footage, or music history.

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. Access to personal data is intended for the owner’s use and authorized operation of these integrations.

Control and retention

The owner can revoke access through Google Account permissions and remove integrations and stored credentials from the connected applications. Operational records and backups may persist until separately deleted under the owner’s retention settings.

Website hosting

This informational site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information under its own privacy statement. This site adds no analytics, advertising, or sign-in forms.

Contact

For questions about this project, contact the operator through the GitHub profile. Do not include private account or device information in public issues.
