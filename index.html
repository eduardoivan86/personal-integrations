Personal Integrations

A personal collection of integrations operated by Eduardo Collazos. The Google OAuth consent configuration is currently named n8n personal.

These integrations connect the owner’s Google services to personally managed tools, including Home Assistant for Nest devices and a personal YouTube Music integration.

Purpose

  • Read Nest thermostat temperature, humidity, and settings, and control the owner’s thermostat when requested.
  • Access authorized Nest camera streams and doorbell or motion events through Home Assistant.
  • Enable personal music functionality using the owner’s authorized YouTube Music account.

This is a personal project, not a service offered to the public. This website contains information about the integrations; it does not provide access to the connected devices or accounts.
